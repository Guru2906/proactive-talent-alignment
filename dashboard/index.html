Scientific Disclosures & Honesty Principles

  • No Fabricated Probabilities: Emergence scores reflect multi-component evidence weights in [0, 1] and are never presented as calibrated class probabilities.
  • Sparse Evidence Disclosure: Discovered clusters with fewer than 40 postings or under 3 quarters of appearance are strictly classified as "Sparse / Low-Evidence" or "Candidate".
  • Separate Forecasting Tasks: Pipeline A predicts macro demand for established canonical roles, while Pipeline B detects uncodified semantic clusters. Neither replaces the other.
  • Curriculum Gap Requirement: Formal syllabus gap evaluation explicitly requires institutional curriculum inputs; current skill gaps are empirical market gaps from active hiring postings.
